VATEL

Humanoid Orchestration for Autonomous Banquet Setup

CDE4301 Innovation & Design Capstone · College of Design and Engineering, National University of Singapore · AY2026/27

The problem

Banquet table setting requires handling diverse, fragile tableware across an environment purpose-built for human reach and access, where fixed automation is impractical and a separate task-specific robot per item type is uneconomical. VATEL develops and evaluates an autonomous perception-and-manipulation pipeline for a humanoid robot to transfer and place banquet tableware, using plate transfer from an AGV as the representative case, with the approach intended to generalise to other tableware handling tasks.

How it works

  1. Plan. The banquet team opens the VATEL Planner on an iPad or a computer. It shows the whole hall with the tables segmented on a 2-D map, and the team plans the event's arrangement there.
  2. Hand off. The planner sends the Unitree R1 humanoid the static map: where each table is, how many sets each table needs, and the position of every set.
  3. Navigate. The R1 navigates the hall from that map, using its Hesai JT128 lidar to find where the tables really are and update the map.
  4. Transfer. It takes plates one at a time off a stack delivered by an AGV and places each at its designated position.
  5. Done. At least 90 % of the plates are in place, and none are broken.
