# Hardware

Two custom parts carry the sensors and their cables on the Unitree R1. Neither one modifies the robot: the helmet
clamps on and screws into the head's own threaded holes, and the cable box hangs from the robot's back handle.

| Item | Version | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sensor helmet (parts P1–P9) | v0.11 | Clamps on the R1's head and carries the Hesai JT128 lidar (tilted 24° forward, on an adapter plate) and the Intel RealSense D435if | Earlier versions printed and fitted on the robot. v0.9–v0.11 reworked the lidar mount for the real JT128 and its right-angle cable plug |
| Cable box (parts B1–B14) | v2.8 | Hangs on the R1's back behind the Jetson. Holds the USB hub, whose USB-C reaches the Jetson, and the lidar's cable splitter. A pulley take-up stores the slack the two head cables need as the head turns | Designed and checked in CAD; not yet built |

## Files

Everything is in `cad/`:

| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| `R1_sensor_helmet_v0.11_ASSEMBLY.step` | the helmet with both sensors and every screw |
| `R1_cable_box_v2.8_ASSEMBLY.step` | the cable box with its hardware, in the same frame as the helmet, so the two open together |
| `parts/` | each part as its own STEP: P = helmet, B = cable box, S = the sensor models |
| `stl_print/` | print-ready STLs, already oriented (z = 0 is the bed); each file name ends with its material |
| `laser_cut/` | the cable box's five 3 mm acrylic panels as 1:1 DXFs, plus all five nested on one sheet |
| `reference/` | Intel's D435 model and the R1 head surface the helmet was fitted to |
| `BOM.md`, `BOM.csv` | bill of materials: what to print, cut and buy, with print settings |
| `README.md` | the full design log: every revision and why, the fits, the build checks and the assembly order |

## Making them

- **Print** the files in `stl_print/`. They're PETG unless the name says otherwise: the pads, the brow bumper and the
  cable clip are TPU, and the crest is PLA for a fit-check (ASA for use on the robot).
- **Laser-cut** `laser_cut/` from 3 mm cast acrylic. Measure the sheet first: the slots assume 2.9–3.1 mm.
- **Buy** the hardware in sections 2 and 2b of `BOM.md`: screws, heat-set inserts, magnets, one NSK 627ZZ bearing and
  a constant-force spring.
- **Assemble** in the order given in the assembly tables of `cad/README.md`.

The CAD is generated by CadQuery scripts. They also check the design: interference with the R1's own meshes across
its joint ranges, screw engagement, and print orientation. The scripts live in the team's working repository, so the
build commands in `cad/README.md` refer to files that are not in this one.
