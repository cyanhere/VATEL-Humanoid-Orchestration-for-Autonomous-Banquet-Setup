# Hardware

CAD for the custom parts on the Unitree R1:

- **Sensor helmet (v0.11):** carries the Hesai JT128 lidar and the Intel RealSense D435if on the robot's head.
- **Cable box (v2.8):** sits on the robot's back, holds the USB hub and takes up the head cables' slack.

`cad/` holds the STEP files, print-ready STLs (`stl_print/`), laser-cut DXFs (`laser_cut/`) and the bill of
materials (`BOM.md`).
